County board cancels permit for 300 MW data center campus

SYNTHETIC FIXTURE — not a real news story. The Madison County board voted 5–2 on Tuesday, February 10, 2026, to cancel the special-use permit for the proposed 300 megawatt data center campus, citing water consumption and noise complaints from residents.

The developer had planned to break ground on privately owned farmland this spring. Board chair Dana Lee said the county "is not closing the door on data centers, but this site was the wrong fit."

It is the third permit cancellation in the region in six months, according to county records.
