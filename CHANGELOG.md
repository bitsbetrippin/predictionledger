## 2.1.1 — 2026-09-18 · UI refresh round 2: usability fixes from the three-journey review

The design handoff's round 2 (evaluated against *new user → first assessment*, *returning user → evidence*, *operator → hold*), implemented on top of 2.1.0. Still no API contract change; one server bookkeeping change (below) that the handoff had left as an open question.

### Changed
- **Prediction detail order**: quote → verdict card → actions → tabs (Evidence first when a verdict exists) → a collapsed *Claim details — dates, modality, geography, components* (`<details>`; it opens while editing). The verdict card carries a **"Read together:"** sentence built from the two fields (`readTogether()` in `PredictionDetail.tsx`: result × time status; sports picks read "Settled by rule from the game record — …"). The Evidence tab opens with "Grouped by component — support must attach to the future claim, in the window" + `?`. Table: the confidence sub-line is gone, the explanation is clamped to two lines, the Sources count is a link that opens the detail on Evidence. Rows are focusable (`tabindex=0`, `aria-selected`): ↑/↓ move the selection (and follow it while the detail is open), Enter opens, Esc closes and returns focus to the row. Deep link `#/predictions?pred=<id>&tab=evidence` (`id` still accepted).
- **Trades order**: mode banner → Arm blockers (paper only) → reconciliation holds → summary tiles → alerts → external holdings → live orders → ledger. Hold cards are two columns: *What this means* + *What the app did* on the left, *What only you can do* as a numbered list in a `--surface-2` box with a warn pinstripe on the right (the topic's sentences, one per step); the intent line names the held reservation and consumed opportunity. Alerts raised by a hold say "raised by the “<hold>” hold above — resolving it is the fix; Acknowledge only hides this row" and the heading counts them. *Resume new orders* is disabled with the reason ("Resume is blocked — n holds open") while any hold is open. Stale tiles carry a STALE chip, warn value colour and warn border. The header shows a PAPER / LIVE / AUTO LIVE tag beside *Trades* with a subtitle such as "Manual live · paused by 2 open holds", and the sidebar Trades row carries the same tag (from `/api/trading/summary`).
- **Library import panel is collapsible**: collapsed by default when the library has videos, open on an empty library; the toolbar buttons open it on the matching tab, a caret collapses it; `#/library?import=<tab>` opens a tab directly — the worked example's "Try it with the fixture transcript" lands on the Transcript tab, which names `fixtures/transcripts/data-center-approvals.srt`.
- **Guided-start strip**: a one-line `role=status` row at the top of the page where the next step happens ("Guided start · step n of 6 · <title>. <why>" + the step's action + ✕). Never on Setup (which has the full section), never on the empty Library (its empty state carries the card), gone once every step derives as done; ✕ sets `dismissedAt`, restart from Setup. Steps map to pages: import/extract → Library, research → Predictions, link → Markets.
- **Accessibility**: one Escape order — popover → drawer → Learn panel → detail (`components/escape.ts`, a priority stack); popovers take focus on open and return it to their trigger; `:focus-visible` ring on summaries and `[tabindex]` too; `prefers-reduced-motion` stops the skeleton pulse and the drawer transition; tertiary text is `#7E8A97` (≥ 4.7:1) everywhere `#6F7B88` was. The Learn page shows "Back to <previous page>"; the Learn panel and popovers never change route, filters, selection or typed notes.
- **Breakpoints are viewport-based**: ≥ 1200 px the detail is a column; 880–1199 px a right overlay (max 560 px) with a backdrop (click or Esc closes); < 880 px drawer navigation, card lists, full-screen panels (`useMediaQuery` + CSS).
- **Server bookkeeping (the handoff's open question, answered from the code):** resolving a hold now closes the alert it raised — `POST /api/trading/intents/:id/resolve-unknown` closes `unknown_submission:<intent>`, `POST /api/trading/holds/:id/resolve` closes the discrepancy / contested-settlement / failed-cancel / stale-sync / stream-gap alert of that hold (`alertKeysForHold` in `services/execution.ts`). Before 2.1.1 only the crash-recovery and external-holding reclassification paths closed alerts with their holds, so an owner-resolved hold left its alert open until acknowledged. Acknowledge still only hides a row. Regressions in `execution.test.ts` (E05) and `review20.test.ts` (RV-04). The other open items: `dispatchBlockers` is `string[]`; `health.version` is `server/package.json` at process start (SETUP §2.6); the verdict guard's rules are G1–G7 as the worked example cites them (`research/verdictGuard.ts`).

## 2.0.0-rc.2 — 2026-09-17 · External holdings, plain-English operations reference, the on-contract gate fix

Follow-up to rc.1 from the owner's first look at the Trades page on a real account: six `discrepancy` holds and two alerts for positions placed by hand on the venue, on markets where the app has no order at all. Still a release candidate (the owner evidence in SETUP §4.16 is unchanged); **no production order was placed.**

### Changed
- **Hand-placed positions are external holdings, not discrepancies (RSK-06).** A venue position on a market where the app has no order of its own is listed under *External holdings* on Trades (net quantity, venue cost basis), counted toward the total / per-market / per-event limits at the venue's cost basis (else $1 per contract), and blocks app entry on that same contract (`no_external_position` gate, `EXTERNAL_POSITION_ON_CONTRACT`). It opens no hold and never pauses the account. Discrepancy holds remain for markets where the app has its own orders. Legacy holds opened by the 1.13 rule for such positions (`"intents":[]`) are resolved on the next reconcile ("reclassified as an external holding"), their alerts closed, an audit event written (`hold.reclassified`); `POST /api/trading/reconcile` reports `externalHoldings` and `reclassifiedHolds`.
- **RV-15 — the on-contract gates compared venue slugs with stored venue ids.** `no_opposing_exposure` and `no_open_order` (RSK-06) filtered the account snapshot by `venueMarketId === contract.venueMarketId`, but the venue keys positions and orders by market *slug* while the contract is compared by venue *id*; when the two differ (they do in production) the gates could never match. The decision service and the preview/submit re-decision now map slugs to venue ids. Found while adding the external gate; covered by `review20` EH-01.
- **Plain-English help on the Trades page.** Every hold and alert shows one sentence of meaning, one sentence of what you do, and a link to the matching section of the new reference; intent chips carry a tooltip; the holds banner explains what a hold is.

### Added
- **`docs/OPERATIONS_REFERENCE.md`** — every summary tile, external holdings, each hold kind (cause, what the app did, what you do), each alert kind, intent / order / position states, pause / disarm / stop / breaker, the decision reason codes, and the 2026-09-17 scenario worked through.
- Test EH-01 (`review20.test.ts`, 194 total): two hand-placed positions (one on a market the app never stored) → external holdings, no discrepancy, a legacy hold + alert reclassified/closed, exposure counts cost basis, app entry on the held contract skipped with `EXTERNAL_POSITION_ON_CONTRACT`, other contracts unaffected until the total-risk cap applies.
- Fake venue positions now carry a cost basis (as the real venue does).

### Migration / rollback
- No migration. `npm install` (version bump) + `npm run build`. Rollback: 2.0.0-rc.1 or 1.14.0 tree; the database is unchanged (resolved holds stay resolved with their note).

## 2.0.0-rc.1 — 2026-09-17 · Release candidate: review fixes, upgrade rehearsal, key-file ACL, soak and qualification reports, full requirement audit

Release candidate for 2.0.0 (requirements OPS-01…05, O01–O07, the FOR-06/07 qualification evidence, and a code review of the 1.13/1.14 execution path). **It stays a release candidate**: the mandatory owner checks (Windows run on real content, the capped live smoke test, the real seven-day paper soak, a production qualification, the upgrade rehearsal on the owner's real 1.9 data) have not been executed, and a production qualification does not exist, so automation cannot be armed anywhere. Every automated test runs against the fake venue; **no production order was placed during development.**

### Fixed (from the 2.0 code review of the execution path — docs/VERIFICATION.md lists each finding with its regression test)
- **RV-01 (P1) — the armed scope is now enforced.** An auto-live decision must fall inside the armed (strategy version, category): `decide()` gains an `authorized_scope` gate (`AUTHORIZATION_SCOPE`), fed from `trading_policy.authorized_*` by the decision service and by the preview/submit re-decision. Before, a production qualification for *any* category made a forecast "qualified" and the scheduler could place an order outside the pair the owner had armed.
- **RV-02 (P1) — a second process's crash recovery cannot corrupt an in-flight submission.** `index.ts` runs `recoverAfterCrash()` only in the process that holds the dispatch lease (deferred until it acquires one); T2 sends nothing unless its marker UPDATE actually moved the row from `reserved`; T3 acknowledges only from `submitting`/`submission_unknown`, and when another process had meanwhile marked the intent unknown, the venue's answer resolves that hold, clears the alert and refreshes the pause; `finishUnsent` is conditional (a concurrent acknowledgement wins, a reservation is released once).
- **RV-03 (P2, release-blocking on a real clock) — freshness ages are no longer negative.** `now` was taken *before* the book/sync fetches, so every real decision saw a book "from the future" and skipped with `BOOK_STALE`. The decision, preview and submit instants are now taken **after** their inputs are gathered (unless a caller pins the clock, as tests do); `decide()` tolerates up to 2 s of skew (`CLOCK_SKEW_TOLERANCE_MS`) and still refuses anything further ahead. The scheduler no longer pins a whole tick to its start time.
- **RV-04 — settlement side cross-check.** The venue does not document whether `positionResolution.side` is the winning side or the account's side. When the venue also reports a realized amount whose sign contradicts the app's reading for our fills, the settlement is *contested*: a `discrepancy` hold (`settlement:<activity>`) pauses new orders and an alert asks the owner to check the venue's statement. The first real settlement (SETUP §4.16) resolves this documented gap.
- **RV-05** — activities are applied oldest-first (the venue lists newest-first), so an original resolution and its correction keep their labels.
- **RV-06** — the daily loss stop buckets settlements by the **budget timezone** (like commitments), not the UTC date of the instant.
- **RV-07 (OPS-02)** — pre-migration backups are scrubbed like manual backups: no `trading.*` secret, no armed grant, binding marked *needs rebind*.
- **RV-08** — orders placed on the website that *sell* now reduce the expected position (`signedFilledQuantity`: action × side), so a manual exit no longer raises a false discrepancy with wrong numbers.
- **RV-09** — the venue's manual/automatic indicator follows who sends the order (owner-confirmed → MANUAL, scheduler → AUTOMATIC), recorded on the preview (`display.origin`).
- **RV-10 / RV-14** — resolving an unknown submission can only link an order on the same contract and side with a known side and matching quantity (`order_mismatch`, `order_side_unknown`).
- **RV-11** — the **newest** production evaluation of a (strategy, category) pair decides: a later failed evaluation revokes qualification (arming refused, forecasts fall back to experimental).
- **RV-12** — a `429` on the create call is **ambiguous** (the venue documents no ordering between throttling and creation and offers no idempotency key): held as `submission_unknown`, never resent. The pinned-SDK test records the new classification.
- **RV-13** — the emergency stop waits (bounded, 25 s) for a POST already in flight before its cancel sweep, so the in-flight order is targeted by the same stop.
- **FOR-06 gate counts distinct events** (found by the O07 harness: 110 decisions covered 28 events and would have passed the "100 events" threshold): `evaluateForecasts` now requires ≥ 100 *distinct* settled events; forecasts without a usable probability are never scored.

## 1.14.0 — 2026-09-17 · Automatic execution behind arming, pause / emergency stop, alerts, the Trades ledger

Fifth release of the Polymarket US track (requirements AUTO-01…05, DASH-01…05, OPS-03/04 controls). **This build can place real orders automatically — only while the owner has armed automation with the exact acknowledgement and the policy hash they reviewed, only for a strategy/category with a production qualification (fixtures never count), and only through the 1.13 preview → send path.** Every automated test runs against the fake venue; **no production order was placed during development.** Automation cannot be armed on a real account until a production qualification exists (≥ 100 settled events with a market baseline), which no data directory has yet — that gate is reported unmet, not waived.

### Added
- **Arming (AUTO-01)** — `POST /api/trading/arm { acknowledge, policyHash, category, strategyVersion? }` (`services/tradingAccounts.ts` `arm`): every gate must hold (validated + fresh account, reconciled binding, a verified contract, a **production** qualification for the exact strategy version and category, the paper rehearsal — ≥ 20 settled US paper positions, pilot default —, the submission and automation features, no holds, not paused, breaker closed), the text must equal `AUTO_LIVE_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT` and `policyHash` must equal the **current** hash (a mismatch means the policy changed under the owner). The authorization stores the hash, strategy and category (migration 016 `trading_policy.authorized_*`); the scheduler refuses whenever the live hash differs; a bare `PUT /api/trading/policy { mode: "auto_live" }` never arms. The policy hash now covers the scheduler budgets too, so any budget edit disarms like a limits edit.
- **Execution scheduler (AUTO-02, `services/autoTrader.ts`)** — its own timer loop started in `index.ts`, never a job on the queue (a slow transcription or research job cannot delay a tick or a cancel; U02 proves it). Per tick, bounded: queue `market.match` for new subscription-sourced picks with no US link, run the computed contract checklist on accepted-but-unverified US links, revalidate verifications older than the window; then evaluate candidates (verified links on open, unresolved contracts with a known cutoff) with a fair per-creator share, an evaluations budget and an orders budget, skipping consumed opportunities, contracts with an open intent, past-cutoff events (never a catch-up bet) and recently evaluated predictions. An eligible auto-live decision (which needs a **qualified** forecast) goes through `execution.preview` → `execution.submit` exactly like a manual order, with `MANUAL_ORDER_INDICATOR_AUTOMATIC`. The gate set is re-read before every single order, so a disarm, pause, hold or lease loss mid-tick stops the tick. Every candidate's outcome is persisted (`automation_runs`, `automation_candidates`), evaluated or not (AUTO-05). Optional **paper autopilot** (`automation.paperAutopilot`) runs the same loop in paper mode for the paper soak.
- **Pause and emergency stop (AUTO-03)** — `POST /api/trading/pause { reason }` / `resume` (owner pause, a dispatch blocker). `POST /api/trading/emergency-stop` (`execution.emergencyStop`): **one statement** disarms and pauses (the 1.13 dispatch marker re-reads that row, so no send can begin after it), then every app-owned non-terminal order is targeted for cancellation with a per-order outcome (`failed_cancel` holds + alerts on failure); positions and history are retained; an in-flight POST is reconciled like any other order. `POST /api/trading/cancel-all { acknowledge }` is a **separately labelled** owner action with its own text (`CANCEL_ALL_ACKNOWLEDGEMENT`) — never the default.
- **Return to disarmed (AUTO-04)** — restart (existing), restore (existing; the backup scrub now also drops the automation authorization), credential change (`connect` disarms), limits or budget edit (hash change), an unknown submission or a reconciliation discrepancy during auto-live (`execution` disarms + alerts), a circuit-breaker opening. Cutoff-passed candidates are skipped before evaluation; recovery marks a restored in-flight intent unknown and never resends.
- **Alerts and circuit breaker (AUTO-05)** — `services/tradingAlerts.ts` (`trading_alerts`, one row per incident key, repeats counted; kinds: unknown submission, disconnection, failed cancel, risk limit, stale sync, discrepancy, resolution, circuit breaker, disarmed, emergency stop; secret-redacted). Breaker in `tradingAccounts` (`recordAdapterOutcome`, state in `trading_accounts.breaker_json`): N consecutive adapter failures (auth, rate limit, network, timeout, 5xx — not 400/404) open it: no new orders, reads and cancels keep working, one alert per incident, disarm; a success after the cooldown closes it; **nothing re-arms by itself**.
- **Trades ledger (DASH-01…05, `services/ledger.ts`)** — `GET /api/trading/summary` (mode, armed kind, pause, account, buying power and sync age from the venue, committed live risk, realized P&L from official settlements, fees, marked unrealized P&L with a stale flag, open/unknown intents, holds, alerts, breaker, lease, stream, last reconcile/tick), `GET /api/trading/ledger?from&to&category&creator&status&mode&reason&external` (one row per decision — skipped included — joined with intent, venue order, position state, official settlement, mark; external orders as labelled rows with no rationale; `Unknown—reconciling` distinct from failed, accepted from filled, partial from full, stale marks from current), `GET /api/trading/ledger.csv` and `.json` (same filters, secret-free), `GET /api/trading/metrics` (OPS-04 counters: decisions, no-trades, intents, read retries, unknown submissions, fills, dropped duplicate events, reconciliation lag, runs, alerts, breaker). ## [1.9.0] — 2026-09-16 — Paper trading
The intermediate step the markets plan called for before real money is ever discussed: a hypothetical position ledger that scores the signals. **The app still never places an order.**

## [1.1.1] — 2026-09-12 — Server type fix from the first clean rebuild
### Fixed
